==============================================
Survey Portal: Survey Based Visibility Setting
==============================================
* Flips the logic of `survey_portal` so that survey answers are by default 
  hidden in the portal
* You can toggle the visibility on for each survey with a new boolean toggle
* Intended for situations where you want to show e.g. survey answers 
  for certain Certificate questionnaires, but don't really care about 
  answers related to event signups, food allergies etc.

Configuration
=============
* Just open a survey and tick "Hide Survey Result in Portal"
* If you installed the module in an existing installation, remember
  to check any old surveys.

Usage
=====
* Log in as a portal user and go to My Account -> Survey Answers
