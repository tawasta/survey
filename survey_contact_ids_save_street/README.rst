===========================
Survey Contacts Save Street
===========================

This module lets a text-answer survey question save the respondent's
answer as a street address, either on the survey participation's own
partner or on one of the survey's contact persons.

Configuration
=============

#. Open a survey question of type **Text Answer**.
#. Check **Save as partner street** to save the answer as the
   participation's partner's street address.
#. Check **Save as contact street** to save the answer as the street
   address of one of the survey's contact persons (pick which contact
   with the **Contact Number** field).
