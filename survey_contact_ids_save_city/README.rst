=========================
Survey Contacts Save City
=========================

This module lets a text-answer survey question save the respondent's
answer as a city, either on the survey participation's own partner or
on one of the survey's contact persons.

Configuration
=============

#. Open a survey question of type **Text Answer**.
#. Check **Save as partner city** to save the answer as the
   participation's partner's city.
#. Check **Save as contact city** to save the answer as the city of one
   of the survey's contact persons (pick which contact with the
   **Contact Number** field).
